# Public Service Covenant (PSC)

## Why PSC Exists

The Public Service Covenant (PSC) was created to bring trust back to public service. We believe that leaders should serve the people, not themselves. Citizens deserve leaders who are honest, open, and focused on building a better future for everyone. PSC exists to set a new standard for public life, where duty always comes before power.

## Our Belief

Public office is a responsibility, not a privilege.

## Our Promise

1. Serve people before politics.
2. Tell the truth.
3. Respect the Constitution.
4. Protect public money.
5. Make decisions openly.
6. Admit mistakes.
7. Listen to citizens.
8. Put duty before power.
9. Reject corruption.
10. Leave public office with honour.

## Candidate Standards

Every PSC candidate voluntarily accepts a higher ethical standard of financial transparency. We expect our candidates to follow these clear rules:

- Declare all public assets before contesting elections.
- Provide an annual public disclosure of all assets.
- Ensure there is no unexplained increase in wealth.
- Maintain complete transparency in all financial interests.
- Publicly disclose any conflicts of interest.
- Ensure family financial transparency where relevant to public duties.
- Maintain ethical conduct before, during, and after holding office.
