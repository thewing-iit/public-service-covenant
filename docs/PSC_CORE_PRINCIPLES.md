# PSC Core Principles

### 1. Public Service Before Power
**Public office exists to serve people.**
Power is a tool to help citizens, not a reward for politicians. Every decision must prioritize the public good over personal or political gain.

### 2. Honesty
**Truth builds trust.**
Honesty is the foundation of public service. Citizens have a right to the truth, even when it is difficult to hear.

### 3. Transparency
**Government should work openly.**
Public business should be conducted in the open. When actions are visible to everyone, it prevents corruption and ensures fairness.

### 4. Accountability
**Every public servant must answer to the people.**
Those who hold office must take responsibility for their actions and mistakes. They must always be ready to explain their decisions to the public.

### 5. Merit
**Leadership should be earned through work and character.**
Positions and responsibilities should be given based on ability and dedication, not on connections or wealth.

### 6. Equal Opportunity
**Every citizen deserves a fair chance to serve.**
The right to participate in building the nation belongs to everyone, regardless of background. We must remove barriers that keep good people out of public life.

### 7. Constitution First
**Every decision must respect the Constitution of India.**
The Constitution is the highest law of the land. All public service must uphold its values of justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity.

### 8. Financial Integrity
**Public office must never be used for personal gain.**
Serving the public is a duty, not a business. Public resources must be protected and used strictly for the benefit of the people.

### 9. Ethical Asset Declaration
**Every PSC candidate must publicly declare assets before contesting.**
Before seeking office, candidates must openly share what they own. This ensures they begin their service with complete honesty.

### 10. Annual Asset Disclosure
**PSC representatives should publish yearly financial declarations.**
Transparency must continue after taking office. Yearly updates allow citizens to ensure that their representatives are not unfairly enriching themselves.

### 11. No Unexplained Wealth
**Any significant increase in wealth must have a lawful and publicly explainable source.**
If a public servant's wealth grows unexpectedly, they must clearly show where it came from to maintain public trust.

### 12. Conflict of Interest
**Private interests must never influence public decisions.**
Public servants must separate their personal affairs from their official duties. If a decision could benefit them personally, they must step aside.

### 13. Family Transparency
**Financial interests involving spouse, dependent children, or controlled entities should be disclosed where relevant to public office.**
Because public duty affects the whole family, financial transparency must extend to close relatives to ensure complete integrity.

### 14. Equal Elections
**Democracy should be a competition of ideas, not money.**
Elections must be fair contests of ability and vision. Wealth should not determine who can serve the nation.

### 15. Responsible Funding
**Every donation must be public. Every expense must be public. Movement funds must never be used for personal benefit.**
Political funding must be completely open. Every rupee received and spent must be visible to everyone, ensuring funds are used only for the public good.

### 16. Citizen Participation
**Good people should not stay silent. A better nation begins when honest citizens step forward to serve.**
Real change requires the active involvement of ordinary citizens. The system only improves when good people decide to take responsibility.

### 17. Long-Term Thinking
**Every policy should help the next generation, not only the next election.**
We must make decisions that build a strong future, rather than focusing only on quick results to win votes today.
